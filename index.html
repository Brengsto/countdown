From your first chart to a full table

Counting against the dealer

Every round is played out, with cards dealt from the shoe. You choose whether you're asked for the count every 3, 5, 8, 10 or 15 hands. A right answer gets a bell, a wrong one sounds like your chips leaving.

Basic strategy

One hand, one dealer card, one decision. Practice everything or just hard totals, soft totals, pairs or deviations. When you get one wrong, Countdown shows you the right play.

EV Simulator

Enter the table rules and your bet spread, then simulate 2 million rounds. You get EV per hour, standard deviation, risk of ruin and N0. The numbers are an approximation, not a promise of winnings.

FAQ

Do I need internet?
Only for the download. The Windows program runs completely offline.
Where is my progress saved?
On your own device. The Windows program sends nothing over the internet.
Is there a Mac app?
Not yet. On a Mac, Countdown runs in your browser with the same exercises, and your progress is saved in that browser.
Is real money involved?
No. The bankroll at the Game Table is play money.
